# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone,
regardless of experience level, background, identity, or opinion. This is a
learning project, so questions and mistakes are normal and welcome.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, especially with beginners.
- Give feedback that is specific, constructive, and about the work.
- Accept feedback gracefully and assume good intent.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or jokes.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated unwanted contact.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space where
someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems to glinboy@live.com. Reports are handled privately and in good
faith. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, warn, or ban people who
violate this code.
